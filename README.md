# Zentora Traders — Sales & Profitability Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview
The Zentora Traders Sales & Profitability Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI. It provides an overview of sales performance, profitability, product categories, sales channels, and country-wise revenue.

The dashboard helps stakeholders monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify top-performing markets, compare revenue channels, and understand monthly sales trends.

## 🎯 Business Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and profitability.
- Identify top-performing countries by revenue.
- Compare revenue contributions across sales channels.
- Evaluate regional revenue distribution.
- Track monthly revenue and gross profit trends.
- Enable interactive analysis using slicers and filters.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query for data transformation and cleaning
- DAX for KPI calculations and business measures
- Data visualization and interactive dashboard design

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

### 1. Country-wise Revenue
- United Kingdom leads with approximately $74K in revenue.
- Germany follows with approximately $70K.
- France, Italy, and Spain are other major revenue contributors.

### 2. Revenue by Sales Channel
- Online Store contributes 40% of revenue share.
- Corporate contributes 28%.
- Retail Partners contributes 18%.
- Marketplace contributes 14%.

### 3. Regional Performance
- Northern Europe represents 31% of the regional comparison.
- Central Europe contributes 30%.
- Western Europe accounts for 20%.
- Southern Europe accounts for 19%.

### 4. Monthly Sales Trends
- June and July show strong revenue performance in the displayed chart.
- February is among the lower-revenue months.
- Comparing revenue and gross profit helps identify periods that may require further investigation.
